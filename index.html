Chicago Business Mix

This tool helps support the understanding of the spatial mix of business types in different areas of the city of Chicago. It allows users to see which areas have a higher or lower concentration of certain types of businesses compared to the city as a whole. The target users include urban planners, business owners, and community organizations interested in understanding the distribution of businesses across Chicago. These users can explore neighborhood business patterns and identify areas where certain types of businesses are more or less represented.

How to use it

Pick a type of business to see which community areas have a higher or lower concentration of that business type compared to the city as a whole.
